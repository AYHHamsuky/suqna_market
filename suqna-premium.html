How it works

Simple as walking
to the market

From search to vendor in three steps. No learning curve. No complicated setup. Just open, search, find.

01
Search in your language
Type in Hausa or English. "Tuwan madara", "swallows", "tuwo" — they all lead to the same vendors. Our engine understands the way Nigerians actually speak.
02
See every vendor on the map
All matching vendors appear as pins. Distance, price, and rating at a glance. Compare and navigate — or just get directions and walk in.
03
Order, chat, or pick up
Place an order, message the vendor, track status — or simply use the directions. Pay securely in-app with Paystack. Your money is protected until delivery.
